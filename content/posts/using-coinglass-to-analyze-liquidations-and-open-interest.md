## Open Interest as a Strength Indicator

Open Interest (OI) represents the total value of all active contracts in the futures market that have not yet been settled or closed. Unlike trading volume, which shows activity over a period, OI demonstrates the inflow or outflow of real money into an asset. When price growth is accompanied by an increase in open interest, it signals the arrival of new buyers and confirms the sustainability of the trend. Conversely, if the price rises while OI falls, the movement is driven by the closing of short positions (a short squeeze), which is often a sign of an imminent reversal. CoinGlass allows you to track this data across specific exchanges, which is critical for identifying manipulation on individual platforms.

## Liquidations as Fuel for Movement

A liquidation is the forced closing of a trader's position by an exchange due to a lack of margin to maintain an open order. In the world of crypto trading, liquidations act as a powerful catalyst for volatility. When the price reaches a cluster of stop-losses and long liquidation levels, a chain reaction occurs: selling triggers a further decline, leading to more liquidations. CoinGlass provides unique real-time liquidation maps and charts. For a professional, a spike in liquidations is not just statistics on retail losses, but a signal of a trend culmination. Often, the most reliable entry points for counter-trend trades are formed exactly during a liquidation cascade.

## The Liquidation Map and Price Magnets

One of the platform's most valuable tools is the Liquidation Heatmap. It visualizes price levels where large volumes of potential liquidations are concentrated. The market seeks liquidity, so these zones act as powerful price magnets. By analyzing the map across different timeframes, a trader can determine in advance where market makers and large algorithms are most likely to steer the price to purge the market of excessive leverage. If you see a dense cluster of liquidations just above the current price, it is a strong argument that the local rally will continue until that volume is captured.

## Long/Short Balance

The Long/Short Ratio tool on CoinGlass helps assess market sentiment among various categories of participants. The platform separates data for top traders and the total number of accounts. A divergence is often observed: the majority of retail traders open shorts, while large players (smart money) accumulate longs. Using this indicator as a contrarian tool allows you to avoid following the crowd. When the long ratio reaches extreme values (for example, above 70%), the market becomes highly vulnerable to a long squeeze, as the slightest price decline will trigger mass position closures.
