## Monitoring Exchange Flows and Liquidity

One of the most highly sought-after sections of Glassnode is Exchange Flows. Tracking the net inflow and outflow of assets to trading venues allows one to assess potential sell-side pressure or, conversely, phases of accumulation. When significant volumes of BTC are withdrawn to cold storage, it signals a reduction in liquid supply, which has historically preceded price appreciation. Glassnode enables real-time monitoring of these processes, providing data on specific exchanges, which helps identify where capital is concentrated and which platforms are setting the trend.

## Cycle Psychology and Investor Behavior

Understanding the psychology of market participants is the key to successful trading. Glassnode Studio offers unique indicators such as NUPL (Net Unrealized Profit/Loss) and SOPR (Spent Output Profit Ratio). These metrics allow you to look into the portfolios of investors to determine if the market is in a state of euphoria or deep depression. For example, when the vast majority of holders are in a state of unrealized profit (high NUPL), the risk of a major correction increases. Conversely, mass realization of losses often marks a market bottom. Using this data allows traders to act counter-cyclically, buying during periods of fear and selling during periods of greed.

## The Power of Custom Calculations in Workbench

For professional analysts, Glassnode has developed the Workbench tool. This is an environment that allows for combining various metrics and creating custom formulas. With it, you can overlay charts, calculate correlations, and build unique oscillators that are not available in the standard set. Workbench turns static analytics into a flexible laboratory where hypotheses are tested against historical data. The ability to export this data via API makes Glassnode Studio a central hub in the ecosystem of automated trading systems and corporate analytical departments.

## Practical Application in Trading

Integrating Glassnode into a trader's daily routine helps minimize the number of false signals. Instead of relying solely on support and resistance levels, an analyst sees the real fundamentals: how many coins were moved, at what price they were acquired, and how long they have remained stagnant (HODL Waves).
