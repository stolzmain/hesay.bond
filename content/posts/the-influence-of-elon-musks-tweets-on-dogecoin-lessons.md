## Tweets as a Driver of Market Liquidity

The mechanics of Musk's influence on the Dogecoin price were built on the element of surprise and a massive influx of retail investors. Each mention of the dog-themed coin on his account triggered a chain reaction. First, trading bots programmed to parse his posts for keywords reacted, creating an instant bullish impulse that was then picked up by a crowd driven by fear of missing out (FOMO). In such moments, trading volume could surge by thousands of percent in a matter of hours. However, these spikes were often driven by a hollow speculative component, unsupported by fundamental changes in the protocol or the infrastructure of the coin itself. This taught traders an important lesson: in the era of digital communications, information spreads faster than the market can rationally evaluate it.

## The Trap of Inflated Investor Expectations

The culmination of Musk's influence was his appearance on Saturday Night Live (SNL) in May 2021. Expectations for this event pushed the price of DOGE to all-time highs exceeding 70 cents. However, the market played out the classic "buy the rumor, sell the news" scenario. A massive sell-off of the asset began during the broadcast, leading to a crushing crash. This episode clearly demonstrated the fragility of assets whose value rests solely on media hype. Many newcomers who entered at the peak of expectations faced liquidations and long-term losses. Professionals, meanwhile, used this period to take profits, understanding that when an asset is being discussed on every corner, its further growth potential is effectively exhausted.

## Crowd Psychology and Algorithmic Trading

Musk's influence on Dogecoin exposed the modern market's deep dependence on the psycho-emotional state of participants. Investors stopped looking at charts, preferring to watch notifications from one specific individual. This created a dangerous precedent of centralized influence within a decentralized industry. Traders who successfully profited from these moves understood that they were not trading cryptocurrency, but "attention." An important takeaway was the realization that in such markets, traditional risk assessment tools work differently. It is critically important to consider the Fear and Greed Index, as well as order execution speed, since a delay of a few seconds could mean the difference between profit and blowing up a deposit.

## Protective Strategies Amidst Chaos

The experience of interacting with the Dogecoin market during Musk's period of activity helped traders develop specific risk management skills. First, it became clear that one should not allocate a significant portion of a portfolio to media-driven assets. Second, the mandatory use of stop-losses became the only way to survive 30-percent drawdowns in five minutes. Experienced market participants learned to filter out the noise, realizing that the impact of tweets weakens over time.
