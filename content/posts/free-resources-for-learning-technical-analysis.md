## Global Knowledge Bases and Encyclopedias

The first entry point for any beginner should be specialized knowledge aggregator portals. Investopedia remains the most authoritative English-language resource. Its technical analysis sections cover hundreds of terms, from simple moving averages to complex Fibonacci levels and Elliott Wave theory. For those who prefer a structured approach ranging from simple to complex, BabyPips is an ideal choice. Despite its historical focus on the forex market, the School of Pipsology free course provides fundamental knowledge on Price Action, crowd psychology, and risk management that is perfectly applicable to any liquid asset, including stocks, commodities, and cryptocurrencies.

## Analytical Platforms and Live Charts

Theory is useless without practice, and here, TradingView is the undisputed leader. It is not just a charting tool but a massive library of free educational content. In the Ideas section, thousands of users share their breakdowns daily, applying various methodologies ranging from classic graphical analysis to Volume Spread Analysis (VSA) and custom indicator strategies. Studying the forecasts of others allows you to see how theoretical knowledge is applied to real-time market situations. Furthermore, the platform offers open access to thousands of user-created scripts, allowing anyone to study the mathematical logic behind trade signals.

## Classic Literature in Open Access

Many recognized trading textbooks that have become the bedrock of modern trading systems are now available in open libraries, on broker educational portals, and on thematic forums. Works by John Murphy on technical analysis of financial markets or Steve Nison on Japanese candlestick charting are mandatory reading. Finding legal digital versions of these manuals allows you to gain an academic understanding of the market. Reading primary sources is critically important, as it helps avoid the distortions and simplifications often found in interpretations by popular bloggers and info-marketers.

## Video Hosting Sites as Interactive Tutorials

YouTube has become the largest free university for traders around the world. Here, it is important to know how to filter content, prioritizing channels from major brokerage houses or recognized analytical agencies that host regular webinars and educational streams. Such resources often break down market mechanics and the specifics of how indicators work without intrusive advertising. Visualizing the process of drawing support and resistance levels in motion helps develop market intuition faster—the skill of instantly recognizing reversal patterns on a chart, which is a key success factor in intraday trading.
