## Technical Superiority Through SQL Queries

The fundamental difference between Dune Analytics and its competitors lies in its openness and flexibility. The platform is built on PostgreSQL and its proprietary Dune SQL engine (Engine V2), which allows users to write custom queries to extract virtually any information from the blockchain. Instead of relying on pre-built reports from proprietary services, an analyst can define their own research parameters. For instance, you can track token distribution among early investors, the minting dynamics of a specific NFT collection, or the volume of fees generated by a particular protocol. The workflow involves parsing and decoding smart contract events, which turns raw transaction hashes into readable function names and arguments. This enables the creation of complex logical structures that combine data from different protocols, which is critical for analyzing the DeFi ecosystem, where protocols often interact with each other like money legos.

## Visualizing Metrics for Accurate Forecasts

The power of Dune Analytics is evident in its ability to turn dry lines of code into interactive dashboards. A professional analyst uses these tools to monitor liquidity and market sentiment in real time. Visualization allows for the immediate detection of anomalies: a sharp outflow of funds from a protocol, a surge in activity on new L2 networks, or a shift in stablecoin dominance. Custom dashboards can include line charts, pie charts, and heatmaps that help visualize the health of a given project. It is important to understand that in the crypto sphere, price often follows fundamental on-chain indicators, such as the number of active addresses or trading volumes on DEXs. By having a dashboard tailored to a specific strategy at hand, a trader gains the ability to get ahead of the curve, seeing the real state of affairs within the blockchain rather than relying on social media news, which are often lagging indicators.
