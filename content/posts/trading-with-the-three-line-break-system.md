## Construction algorithm and key rules

The mechanics of building TLB charts are based on comparing the current closing price with the extremes of the previous lines. If the price closes above the high of the previous white (bullish) line, a new white line is drawn. If the price falls below the low of the previous black (bearish) line, a new black line is drawn. However, the key rule that gave the system its name comes into play during a trend reversal. For a line of the opposite color to appear on the chart, the price must exceed the extremes of the last three consecutive lines. This creates a specific sensitivity threshold: the market must prove the seriousness of its intention to change the trend before the chart registers it.

## Specifics of trend reversal identification

The three-line system acts as a high-frequency filter. When there is a strong trend in the market, the chart obediently draws a series of lines of the same color. But as soon as volatility increases or a correction begins, the system requires confirmation. If we see three white lines in a row, the closing price must fall below the low of the very first line of that trio for a black line to form. This three-line rule effectively protects the trader from premature exits during minor pullbacks. It is important to understand that during strong directional moves, the lines can be very short or, conversely, abnormally long, which visually suggests the strength of the current momentum.

## Advantages of filtering market noise

The main advantage of using Three Line Break is psychological comfort and the clarity of graphical analysis. In classic candlestick charts, a trader often panics when seeing alternating red and green candles within a sideways channel. In such moments, TLB may not draw any new lines at all, signaling that the market situation has not fundamentally changed. This makes the system ideal for trend-following strategies. The tool allows you to hold a profitable position as long as possible, ignoring short-term corrections that fail to break the levels of the previous three periods. Thus, one of the main problems of trading—premature profit taking—is solved.

## Entry and exit strategies

The classic signal in the TLB system is quite simple: a buy order is executed upon the appearance of the first white line after a series of black ones, and a sell order is executed upon the appearance of the first black line after a series of white ones. However, experienced analysts recommend waiting for confirmation. Since the chart is derived from price and possesses a lag, the entry occurs at the moment the reversal line forms. It is logical to place a stop-loss in such a system beyond the extreme of the reversal block. If the trend is confirmed, the position is held until a line of the opposite color appears. This allows you to capture the meat of the trend, sacrificing only a small portion of profit at the reversal.
