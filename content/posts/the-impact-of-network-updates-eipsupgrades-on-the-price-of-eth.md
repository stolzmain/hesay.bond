## The Economic Revolution of EIP-1559

The introduction of the fee-burning mechanism as part of the London hard fork was a turning point in the asset's valuation. Prior to this, ETH was a purely inflationary instrument. With the advent of EIP-1559, the correlation between on-chain activity and asset price has strengthened radically. Now, every launch of a popular NFT collection or surge in trading volume on decentralized exchanges directly reduces the circulating supply. For analysts, this signifies a transition to an ultrasound money model. During periods of high network congestion, the protocol burns more tokens than it issues, creating a scarcity that has historically acted as a powerful driver for price growth in the long term.

## The Global Shift to Staking

The Merge update eliminated mining, reducing the issuance of new ETH by approximately 90%. This event is often called a triple halving, as its effect on supply is comparable to three reward-reduction cycles on the Bitcoin network. From a professional trading perspective, the transition to Proof-of-Stake completely changed the seller structure: miners with their high operational expenses have been replaced by stakers, who are inclined toward holding the asset long-term. This has significantly reduced constant sell pressure on the order book. Furthermore, staking has turned ETH into a yield-bearing asset, allowing institutional capital to view it through the lens of classical financial yield metrics.

## Lowering Barriers via Dencun

Recent large-scale updates, such as Dencun with the implementation of EIP-4844 (Proto-Danksharding), are focused on ecosystem scalability. At first glance, the tenfold reduction in transaction costs on Layer 2 (L2) networks might seem like a negative factor due to the decrease in the volume of ETH burned in the short term. However, deep analysis suggests the opposite: the radical reduction in costs for operations attracts millions of new users and the corporate sector. The growth of the L2 ecosystem exponentially increases the Total Value Locked (TVL) and the number of active addresses, which strengthens Ethereum's dominance as a base settlement layer and ultimately increases the intrinsic value of the underlying asset.

## Psychology and Update Cycles

Market dynamics surrounding updates often follow the classic buy the rumor, sell the news pattern. In the months leading up to a major hard fork, there is usually a steady inflow of capital and an increase in ETH dominance relative to the rest of the market. Speculative interest peaks a few days before the event, which often leads to local overheating. However, after the successful implementation of changes, a technical correction frequently follows as short-term players take profits. Understanding this cyclicity allows professional traders to effectively leverage volatility, clearly distinguishing between speculative opportunities and fundamental structural changes in the token's economy.

## Institutional Adoption and Risks

Each update consistently lowers technical risks and makes the network more predictable for regulators and large investment funds.
