Capital first accumulates in Bitcoin, boosting its dominance, then flows into Ethereum and high-liquidity assets (Large Caps), and is subsequently distributed across highly specialized sectors.

Professional traders use rotation to stay in strong coins that demonstrate outperformance relative to the rest of the market. Understanding this liquidity waterfall allows traders to avoid drawdowns in stagnant projects and enter trending narratives during the early stages of momentum formation.

## Major asset groups and narratives

To perform effective analysis, the market must be segmented into clear categories. Today, these include infrastructure Layer 1s (Solana, Avalanche), Layer 2 scaling solutions (Arbitrum, Optimism), Artificial Intelligence (AI), Real World Assets (RWA), Decentralized Finance (DeFi), and the memecoin sector. Each sector has its own triggers. For example, news from major tech companies often sparks a rally in AI tokens like Near or Render, while an Ethereum protocol upgrade can trigger a rally in the L2 ecosystem.

The key to success lies in identifying the leading narrative. It is crucial for a trader to track which sector shows the most resilience during Bitcoin dips. If coins in a specific segment are bought up faster than others during a market correction, it is a clear sign that this is where the main interest of large buyers is concentrated. Focusing on 3–5 top assets within a leading sector provides a significantly higher probability of a profitable trade than trying to cover the entire market at once.

## Technical markers for capital rotation

To spot the beginning of a rotation in time, you must analyze the Bitcoin Dominance index (BTC.D). Its decline against the backdrop of a stable or rising BTC price is a classic signal of the start of an altseason. However, modern cycles have become more fragmented. We now see sector-specific seasons more often rather than a broad, universal rally for all coins. An additional indicator is the Total 3 chart (market capitalization excluding BTC and ETH). An increase in this metric during local breakouts of specific sector charts confirms the inflow of fresh capital.

It is also critically important to monitor the relative strength of assets. Comparing the chart of a specific coin against SOL/BTC or ETH/BTC pairs helps determine if the asset is receiving a liquidity inflow or simply passively following the market. Spikes in trading volume within a specific group of coins while average volume remains flat in other segments is a primary sign that smart money has started building positions in a new direction.

## Strategy for finding correlated assets

The most effective tactic within sector analysis is the catching-up asset method. Within each sector, there are leaders (Beta) and laggards. When a sector flagship, such as Solana, shows a 20% gain, liquidity inevitably begins to flow into the ecosystem's internal projects: decentralized exchanges, lending protocols, and oracles. If you missed the leader's move, do not chase the departing train.
