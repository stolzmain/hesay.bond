## Market Psychology and Liquidity

Why do session extremes act as such a powerful magnet for price? The answer lies in the mechanics of liquidity distribution. Most retail traders, along with many algorithmic systems, place their protective stop orders directly beyond local highs and lows. For institutional players—banks and hedge funds—these zones become magnets. To execute large buy or sell orders without significant slippage, they require counter-liquidity, which is concentrated precisely in these stop-loss clusters. Understanding where session boundaries lie allows a trader to identify zones of potential turbulence, helping them avoid becoming exit liquidity for market makers and instead allowing them to follow the smart money.

## Specifics of Trading Sessions

Every trading session has a unique character that must be accounted for when analyzing the indicator. The Asian session is often characterized by low volatility and the formation of a narrow price range. Asian highs and lows frequently serve as benchmarks for the London Breakout—a sharp impulse at the start of European trading. The European session typically brings the bulk of market liquidity and often sets the directional bias for the entire trading day. The American session, in turn, can either confirm the existing trend or trigger a sharp reversal, especially if the price hits the extremes of previous days. Using the indicator allows for the visualization of these market phases, helping the trader adapt their strategy to the current context.

## Breakout Trading Strategy

One of the most popular methods for utilizing this indicator is trading the breakout of the session range. If the price decisively crosses a session high on increased volume, it signals buyer dominance and a high probability of a continued bullish impulse. However, experienced pros rarely chase the price. They seek confirmation in the form of a retest: when the price returns to the broken boundary to test its strength. At this moment, yesterday's resistance becomes today's support. It is crucial to remember that false breakouts are common, so using supplementary tools like volume or Price Action candlestick patterns is a mandatory requirement to filter out market noise and protect your capital.

## False Breakouts and Liquidity Sweeps

Experienced traders often use session boundaries to hunt for counter-trend opportunities. A liquidity sweep pattern occurs when the price briefly tags a session high or low, triggers participant stop-losses, and then sharply reverses back inside the range. This is a clear sign of weakness in the current move and an absence of real demand beyond the level.
