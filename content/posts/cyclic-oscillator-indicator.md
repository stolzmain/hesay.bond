## Understanding the market cycle concept

At the core of the Cyclic Oscillator lies the hypothesis that market movements consist of several overlapping cycles of varying durations. These can range from short-term intraday fluctuations to long-term seasonal or economic cycles. The primary task of the indicator is to isolate the dominant cycle of the current moment. Unlike classic oscillators that measure the rate of price change, this cyclic tool focuses on identifying the cycle phase: whether the market is in a growth stage, hitting a peak, or entering a decline phase. This allows a trader to not just follow the price, but to anticipate reversal points before they become obvious to the majority of market participants.

## Mathematical foundation and mechanics

The mechanics of the Cyclic Oscillator are based on the process of detrending, which involves removing the long-term trend component from the price chart. This allows for a focus on pure price oscillations relative to a central axis. Mathematically, this is often implemented via band-pass filters or Fourier transforms, which cut off high-frequency noise and low-frequency trend shifts. As a result, the trader sees a smooth sinusoidal line oscillating between predefined levels. The most critical setting here is the cycle period. If the period is chosen incorrectly, the indicator will provide lagging signals, which is why professionals use adaptive versions of the oscillator that adjust to changing volatility and the duration of the current market cycle.

## Identifying accumulation and distribution phases

The Cyclic Oscillator is perfectly suited for visualizing capital accumulation and distribution cycles. When the indicator line is in the lower extreme zone, it signals the end of a sell-off cycle and the likely start of an accumulation phase. At this moment, whales begin building positions as bearish pressure exhausts itself. Conversely, reaching the upper boundaries indicates an overheated market and a transition to a distribution phase, where smart money starts taking profits. The key difference here is that the Cyclic Oscillator holds a signal in the extreme zones only if the cycle has not yet completed, which protects the trader from entering prematurely against a strong impulse.

## Zero-line crossover signals

One of the most reliable trading signals is the oscillator crossing the zero (central) line. This event marks a shift in the dominant market force. When the line crosses zero from bottom to top, it confirms a shift into an upward cycle phase, which serves as a strong buy signal (Long). A cross from top to bottom indicates the start of a downward phase and the opportunity to open short positions (Short). The advantage of the Cyclic Oscillator in this regard is its smoothness; it generates fewer whipsaws—those chains of false crossovers that are typical for more sensitive indicators during periods of price consolidation.
