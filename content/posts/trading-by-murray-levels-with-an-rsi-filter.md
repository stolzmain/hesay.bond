Murray divided the price range into eight equal intervals (octaves), with each level performing a specific role, ranging from powerful support to zones of uncertainty. ## Psychology and Significance of Key Lines

Each line in the Murray grid has a psychological foundation. The 4/8 level is considered the primary equilibrium point or waterline; if price is above it, the market is viewed as bullish, and if below, bearish. The 0/8 and 8/8 levels are the strongest reversal points; these act as trading channel boundaries, where a breakout is rare and often signals that an asset is oversold or overbought. The 1/8 and 7/8 lines serve as weak stop levels, where price may consolidate before continuing its move. It is crucial for a trader to understand that Murray levels are not merely lines on a chart, but zones where the interests of major institutional players collide. Working with them requires patience, as price often tests these boundaries multiple times before establishing a sustainable impulse.

## The Role of the RSI Oscillator in the System

In this strategy, the Relative Strength Index (RSI) acts as a critical filter, allowing traders to weed out false breakouts of Murray levels. A Murray level alone may indicate a potential entry point, but only the RSI confirms the presence of the necessary momentum or the exhaustion of the current trend. We utilize a standard period of 14, where zones above 70 and below 30 signal critical market states. When price approaches a strong Murray level (e.g., 1/8 or 0/8) while the RSI is in the oversold zone and begins to turn upward, it creates a powerful cumulative buy signal. Without RSI confirmation, entering a trade at a level is considered premature, as price may pierce through the level during a strong directional move.

## Algorithm for Opening Long Positions

To open a long position, a trader must wait for two conditions to be met. First, the price must touch or briefly dip below the 0/8 or 1/8 levels. These zones are considered the most favorable for finding a bottom. The second condition: the RSI indicator must drop below 30 and then begin to exit the oversold zone to the upside. An ideal signal is the formation of a bullish divergence on the RSI at the moment of testing the Murray level, which indicates that selling pressure is waning despite low prices. Entry is executed at the close of the candle that confirms the bounce from the level. The first Take Profit target is the median 4/8 line, which often attracts price like a magnet following a deep correction.

## Mechanics for Entering Short Positions

Short sales are executed on the inverse principle. The trader looks for situations where the asset reaches the upper boundaries of the grid — the 7/8 or 8/8 levels. These are zones of extreme optimism where buyers begin to book profits and major players open short positions. A signal is considered valid if, at the moment of touching the Murray level, the RSI indicator is above 70, signaling an overheated market.
