## Popular services for transaction tracking

The most accessible and effective way to obtain information on large trades is through specialized aggregators. One of the most famous is Whale Alert, which broadcasts transaction data in real time via social networks and Telegram channels. There are also platforms like WhaleStats, which focus on analyzing top wallets on the Ethereum, Polygon, and BSC networks. For deeper analysis, professionals use ClankApp or WhaleMap. These services aggregate data directly from blockchains, filtering out small transfers and leaving only those that exceed a specific threshold (e.g., $1 million or more). Using such tools allows a trader to avoid monitoring blockchain explorers manually, receiving ready-to-use, structured information instead.

## Technical setup for Telegram notifications

For most traders, the most convenient format is notifications via Telegram. To set up a personalized data stream, you can use bots such as Whale Alert Bot or EtherDROPS. The setup process usually involves several steps. First, you need to select the networks of interest (BTC, ETH, Solana, and others). Then, a minimum transaction threshold is set: for Bitcoin, this could be amounts from 500 BTC, and for altcoins, the equivalent in dollars. It is important to configure the types of notifications: transfers from a wallet to an exchange (potential sell pressure), from an exchange to a wallet (accumulation), or between exchanges (arbitrage or liquidity provision). Proper configuration allows you to receive only relevant signals without overloading your information flow with noise.

## Using on-chain analysis tools

If standard bots are not enough, professional analysts resort to direct on-chain monitoring tools such as Etherscan, BscScan, or Solscan. These explorers have a Watch List feature. You can find the addresses of the most active wallets or those belonging to major funds (e.g., Jump Crypto or a16z) and subscribe to alerts for any balance changes. When a transaction occurs, the system automatically sends an email or push notification. Platforms like Arkham Intelligence and Nansen are also extremely useful. They allow you to visualize connections between wallets and tag their ownership to specific entities, giving the trader insight into who is actually making the trade—a market maker, an exchange, or a private investor.

## Filtering noise and setting limits

The key problem when working with Whale Alerts is information overload. If you set the notification threshold too low, a trader will receive hundreds of messages per hour, most of which have no impact on the market. Experienced analysts recommend segmenting alerts. For large-cap assets (BTC, ETH), it is worth focusing on trades starting from $10-50 million. For mid-cap assets, transactions from $500,000 to $1 million can be significant.
