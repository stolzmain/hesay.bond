The essence of the process lies in a rapid price increase driven not so much by an organic influx of new buyers, but by the forced closing of short positions. When the price begins to rise, traders betting on a decline (bears) face the necessity of buying back the asset at market prices to cover losses or due to automatic margin calls. This creates a snowball effect: forced purchases generate a new wave of growth, which in turn triggers the next wave of liquidations.

## Mechanics of Price Impulse Formation
For a high-quality squeeze to form, a critical mass of bearish positions and a high concentration of leverage are required. In altcoins, this often happens after a long and grueling downtrend, when most market participants become accustomed to constant drops and begin opening short positions at local lows. The higher the leverage used by sellers, the closer their liquidation levels are to the current market price. In such an overheated situation, even a small upward impulse, caused by a large player buying or moderately positive news, can activate a chain of stop-losses. It is important for a professional analyst to understand that a short squeeze is a temporary technical imbalance rather than a fundamental change in the project's value, and one must know how to monetize this period of market inefficiency in time.

## Monitoring Key Derivatives Metrics
The first and most reliable signal of a possible squeeze is a negative funding rate in the futures markets. If the funding rate drops deep into the negative zone, it means that sellers are dominant and are paying buyers to hold their positions. This is a clear sign of an overcrowded bearish side. A second critical tool is Open Interest. If the asset price consolidates or begins to rise slowly against the backdrop of a sharp increase in open interest with a negative funding rate, it means the market is accumulating fuel for a future explosion. Using liquidation heatmaps allows for the precise identification of price zones where the largest number of short-seller stop orders are concentrated, the reaching of which will trigger a chain reaction of market order execution.

## The Role of Volume and Chart Structure
Technical preparation for a squeeze in altcoins often looks like a prolonged base or consolidation below an important resistance level. While retail traders continue to open sell positions, expecting a pullback, a market maker or large capital accumulates a position, holding the price in a narrow range. A breakout of key resistance on high volume serves as a classic trigger for entry. In low-cap coins, the absence of dense bids in the order book leads to market purchases from liquidations piercing price levels one after another, causing a vertical spike in quotes within minutes. Volume analysis in this context confirms the validity of the buyers' intent and the exit of trapped sellers.

## Entry and Trade Management Strategy
The optimal entry point into a position is the moment of a true breakout of a local high, confirmed by a sharp drop in open interest, which directly indicates a massive exit of shorts from the market. Trading such moves requires lightning-fast reactions and a clear plan.
