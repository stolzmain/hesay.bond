The first gap is an exhaustion gap, which occurs in the direction of the current trend and points to a final, often emotional, spike in buying or selling activity. The second is a breakaway gap, which occurs in the opposite direction, confirming a trend reversal. In the crypto context, this pattern is rarer than in traditional stock markets due to the 24/7 nature of trading; however, on Bitcoin futures charts (CME) and during periods of extreme volatility on spot exchanges, it possesses exceptional predictive power.

## Bearish scenario at the market top

A bearish island reversal forms at the peak of an uptrend and is a harbinger of a deep correction or the beginning of a long-term downtrend cycle. The asset price makes a sharp jump upward, leaving an unfilled space, consolidates in a narrow range over several candles, and then drops downward with a new gap. This indicates that buying demand is completely exhausted and the initiative has been seized by large institutional players taking profit. In the cryptocurrency market, such situations are often observed after parabolic rallies in Bitcoin or leading altcoins. The isolated "island" of prices becomes a trap for late buyers who have succumbed to the fear of missing out (FOMO). A clear example can be seen on charts of mid-cap assets, where a sudden outflow of liquidity leads to gaps even on higher timeframes.

## Bullish formation in the accumulation zone

A bullish island reversal appears at the bottom of a downtrend and signals that the market has reached a floor. Here, the first gap is directed downward, symbolizing a panic sell-off and the final capitulation of the remaining holders. After a short period of stagnation or the formation of a "doji," the price "jumps" upward, leaving the zone of low prices behind. For a professional trader, this is a powerful buy signal, as it indicates an aggressive absorption of supply by "smart money." In the price history of Ethereum and other fundamental projects, such patterns have often preceded prolonged growth cycles. It is important to understand that an "island" can consist of either a single candle or a whole cluster of bars, but the key factor remains the symmetry of the levels where the price gaps occurred, which creates the visual isolation.

## The role of trading volume and confirmation

To confirm the validity of an island reversal in cryptocurrencies, it is critically important to analyze volume dynamics. In an ideal scenario, the first gap is accompanied by moderate volume, while the second gap, which initiates the reversal, should occur on a significant surge in activity. This confirms the influx of new capital and the decisiveness of market makers. If the second gap is not backed by volume, there is a high risk of a fakeout and a subsequent "gap fill," which invalidates the pattern. Traders should use additional tools, such as the Relative Strength Index (RSI) or a stochastic oscillator, to identify convergences or divergences that often accompany the formation of an "island" and increase the probability of a successful trade.

## Practical application and risk management

Trading this pattern requires strict discipline and quick decision-making. Entering a position is usually carried out immediately after the second gap is locked in or on a small technical pullback to its boundary.
